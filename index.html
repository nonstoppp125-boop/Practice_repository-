Workflow

My Data Analytics Approach

The eight steps I follow on every project, from question to recommendation.

  1. Understand the business problem

    Learn what decision needs support and why it matters.

  2. Define questions and metrics

    Turn the problem into specific questions and measurable indicators.

  3. Collect and inspect data

    Gather the data and check its structure, size, and gaps.

  4. Clean and validate data

    Fix errors, handle missing values, and confirm the data can be trusted.

  5. Analyze patterns and relationships

    Look for trends, comparisons, and connections that answer the questions.

  6. Visualize findings

    Use clear charts and tables so results are easy to understand.

  7. Communicate insights

    Explain what was found in plain language, including limits.

  8. Recommend evidence-based actions

    Suggest next steps that are supported by the data.
